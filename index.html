About us

A Zambian security partner you can rely on

G Force Security has protected people and assets across Zambia for over two decades, growing from its first roster of officers in Lusaka into one of the country’s most respected private security providers.

Today our trained, vetted officers and specialist teams operate everywhere from the Copperbelt to the Eastern Province, serving banks, mines, government institutions, diplomatic missions and residential clients with the same discipline and care.

  • National footprint with branches in 7 major towns
  • In-house training, screening and vetting of all officers
  • Mission: integrity, reliability and technology in everything we do

Our services

Complete security solutions, under one roof

From guarding and risk advice to cash logistics and electronic security, we design protection that fits your operation.

Security Guarding

Uniformed static and mobile guarding for offices, retail, industrial and residential sites, supported by supervision, monitoring and incident response.

Risk Management & Consulting

Security audits, threat assessments and custom protection plans that help you understand exposure and strengthen your defences before issues arise.

Cash-in-Transit & Cash Solutions

Armoured cash movement, ATM replenishment and managed cash services delivered with strict procedure, tracking and accountability.

Electronic Security

CCTV, intruder alarms, access control and remote monitoring that keep you informed and in control, 24 hours a day.
